Malls Slot Parking Reservation

About PARKEASE

Parking plans that start before you reach the mall.

Malls Slot Parking Reservation helps drivers find parking at participating malls and gives mall teams a shared place to manage spaces and arrivals.

What the website does

Drivers can browse registered malls, check the parking spaces listed for each location, and reserve an available slot for a chosen date and time. After a successful reservation, the system provides a QR code for the staff to scan at entry. Mall administrators and staff use separate accounts and tools to keep parking information up to date.

A simple flow from search to arrival

1

Choose your role

Use the user account to book parking, or sign in through staff/admin access to manage a mall.

2

Find a participating mall

Browse available malls and open a location to see its parking slots and compatibility details.

3

Reserve a suitable slot

Select an available space, enter your vehicle and visit details, and submit the reservation.

4

Show your reservation QR

Present the generated QR code when you arrive. Staff scan it to verify the reservation and mark the slot as occupied.

Tools for each role

U

Visitors

Create or sign in to a user account, browse malls, reserve parking, view bookings, and present the reservation QR code on arrival.

A

Mall administrators

Manage staff accounts and parking-slot setup, review the slot grid, and update slot availability and status for their mall.

S

Mall staff

View the live parking grid, update a slot when vehicles arrive or leave, and scan reservation QR codes at entry.
